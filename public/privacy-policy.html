Privacy Policy

SwiftSell — Last updated August 26, 2026

SwiftSell ("the app") is a business management tool for tracking inventory, orders, customers, and expenses. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what access is requested from your Google Account.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, SwiftSell receives your basic profile information:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your Google account profile picture (if available)

This information is used solely to identify your account within SwiftSell and is stored by our database provider, Supabase, associated with your unique account ID.

Separately, you may choose to manually enter business data into the app, including:

  • Product, inventory, and pricing details
  • Customer names and contact details you provide
  • Order and transaction records
  • Expense records

This data is entered by you and is never accessed, read, or shared by us beyond storing it for your own use of the app.

Google Sheets Access (Optional Feature)

SwiftSell includes an optional "Export to Sheets" feature. If you use it, the app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets permission from Google. This permission is used only at the moment you click "Export to Sheets" on the Finances page, and only to:

  • Create a new, single spreadsheet in your Google Drive
  • Write your expense records (date, category, description, amount) into that new spreadsheet

SwiftSell does not read, list, modify, or delete any other spreadsheet or file in your Google Drive. This permission is never used in the background — only when you explicitly trigger an export.

How Your Data Is Stored

Your account and business data are stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database with Row Level Security enabled, meaning your data is only readable and writable by your own authenticated account — not by other users of the app, and not by us in the normal course of operating the service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or business data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your data is used exclusively to provide the app's functionality to you.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact the developer at the email address below.

Third-Party Services

SwiftSell relies on the following third-party providers to operate:

  • Google — for account sign-in and, optionally, spreadsheet export
  • Supabase — for authentication and database hosting
  • Vercel — for application hosting
